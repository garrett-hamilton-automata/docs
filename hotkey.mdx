title: "Set a custom global keyboard shortcut for PinPoint"
description: "Change PinPoint's global hotkey from the default Alt+P to any key combination. Learn how to set a new shortcut and what combinations work."
---

PinPoint's global hotkey is the fastest way to pin or unpin any window — one keypress from anywhere on your desktop, no clicking required. The default is **Alt+P**, but you can change it to any combination that fits your workflow. This page explains how to set a new hotkey and what makes a combination valid.

## Default hotkey

PinPoint ships with **Alt+P** as the default hotkey. Pressing it toggles Always-on-Top on whichever window currently has focus.

## How to change your hotkey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Right-click the PinPoint icon in the system tray and choose **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Hotkey field">
    At the top of the settings form, locate the **Hotkey** row. The field shows your current hotkey combination.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the hotkey field">
    Click the field. It updates to show **(click to change)**, indicating it's ready to capture a new combination.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press your new combination">
    With the field focused, press the key combination you want to use. PinPoint records it immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Settings save automatically. The field now displays your new hotkey. You can test it right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What makes a valid hotkey

Not every key combination is accepted. A valid PinPoint hotkey must follow these rules:

- **At least one modifier key** — your combination must include **Ctrl**, **Alt**, **Shift**, or the **Windows key** (Super).
- **One non-modifier key** — combine your modifier(s) with a single letter, number, or function key (for example, `P`, `9`, or `F9`).

**Valid examples:**

- `Ctrl+Alt+P`
- `Shift+F9`
- `Alt+Shift+W`
- `Windows+P`

<Warning>
  Choose a hotkey that doesn't conflict with shortcuts in apps you use frequently. For example, **Ctrl+Z** is undo in most apps — avoid it. Similarly, avoid **Ctrl+C**, **Ctrl+V**, and other universal shortcuts.
</Warning>

## Resetting to default

To go back to **Alt+P**, open Settings, click the hotkey field, and press **Alt+P**. PinPoint saves it immediately.

<Tip>
  If your new hotkey doesn't seem to work, another application may be capturing that combination globally. Try a different combination — adding an extra modifier key (for example, switching from `Ctrl+P` to `Ctrl+Shift+P`) often resolves conflicts.
</Tip>
