PinPoint's global hotkey is the keyboard shortcut you press to pin or unpin the focused window. The default is **Alt\+P**, but you can change it to any combination you prefer.

## How to change your hotkey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Right-click the PinPoint tray icon and choose **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the hotkey field">
    Click the field. It updates to show **(click to record)** to indicate it is listening for your new combination.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press your new combination">
    Press the key combination you want to use — for example, **Ctrl\+Shift\+P**. The field updates immediately to show the combination you pressed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The hotkey is saved automatically. Close Settings or send PinPoint to the tray — your new hotkey is active immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Hotkey rules

- Your hotkey must include at least one modifier key (**Ctrl**, **Alt**, **Shift**, or **Win**).
- Global hotkeys are exclusive — only one app can hold a combination at a time. If another app has already registered the same combination, PinPoint's hotkey will not work.
- The Windows key (**Win**) can be used in your hotkey combination. However, many Win\+key combinations are reserved by Windows (for example, Win\+E). If you choose a combination that Windows already claims, it will intercept the keypress before PinPoint can.

<Tip>
  If your hotkey stops working, open Settings and re-record it. This re-registers the combination with Windows.
</Tip>
