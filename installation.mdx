This page walks you through downloading and installing PinPoint on your Windows machine. The process takes under a minute, and PinPoint is ready to use as soon as the installer finishes.

## System requirements

- **Windows 10 or Windows 11** (64-bit)
- No additional software or runtime required

## Install PinPoint

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the installer">
    Get the PinPoint MSI installer from one of two places:

    - **Automata Labs website** — visit [automatalabs.ca](https://automatalabs.ca) and download the latest release
    - **Microsoft Store** — search for PinPoint in the Microsoft Store and click **Get**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the MSI installer">
    Open the downloaded `.msi` file and follow the standard Windows installer wizard. Accept the license agreement, choose an install location if prompted, and click **Install**. Windows may ask for permission to make changes to your device — click **Yes** to continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch PinPoint">
    PinPoint starts automatically when the installer finishes. You'll see the PinPoint icon appear in your Windows system tray (the notification area at the right end of the taskbar).

    <Tip>
      If you don't see the tray icon, check the hidden icons area (the **^** arrow in the taskbar corner).
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the welcome screen">
    The first time PinPoint launches, a welcome screen appears. It shows your default hotkey (**Alt+P**) and explains how the system tray works. Click **Acknowledge & Send to Tray** to dismiss it — PinPoint moves to the background and your hotkey is active immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  PinPoint adds itself to Windows startup automatically so it's ready whenever you log in. You can turn this off in **Settings**.
</Note>

## What you'll see after installing

Once setup is complete:

- The **PinPoint icon** appears in your system tray. Right-click it at any time to see pinned windows or open Settings.
- Your **default hotkey** is **Alt+P**. Focus any window and press it to pin that window to Always-on-Top.
- A **colored border** appears around any pinned window as a visual indicator.
