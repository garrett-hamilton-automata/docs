---
title: "Using the PinPoint system tray to manage pinned apps"
description: "Use the PinPoint system tray icon to see all your pinned windows, unpin individual windows or all at once, and open Settings or quit the app."
---

PinPoint lives entirely in the Windows system tray. You don't need to open a main application window to use it — the tray icon gives you a full view of every pinned window and lets you unpin, configure, or close the app without interrupting your workflow. This page covers how to find the tray icon and everything you can do from its menu.

## Finding the tray icon

The PinPoint icon sits in the system tray at the bottom-right corner of the Windows taskbar, next to the clock. If you don't see it, Windows may be hiding overflow icons. Click the **^** (chevron) arrow next to the tray to expand the overflow area — PinPoint's icon will be there.

To keep the icon always visible, drag it from the overflow area onto the main taskbar tray.

## Tray menu options

Right-click the PinPoint tray icon to open the menu. The menu contains the following items:

| Menu item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **[Window title]** | Each currently-pinned window appears here by title. Click a title to unpin that specific window. |
| **Unpin All** | Removes Always-on-Top from every pinned window at once. |
| **Settings** | Opens the PinPoint settings window. |
| **Quit** | Closes PinPoint completely. Your global hotkey stops working until you relaunch the app. |

<Note>
  Window titles in the tray menu are sorted alphabetically and truncated to 40 characters if they're longer.
</Note>

## Opening settings from the tray

Right-click the tray icon and choose **Settings** to open the settings window. You can also try double-clicking the tray icon, though this behavior may vary depending on your version of Windows and system configuration.

From Settings you can change your hotkey, adjust the border color and thickness, and configure other preferences. All changes save automatically.

<Tip>
  Use **Unpin All** before switching tasks or going into a presentation to quickly clear all your pinned windows.
</Tip>

## When PinPoint is not in the tray

If the PinPoint icon is missing from both the main tray and the overflow area, PinPoint is probably not running. Your global hotkey won't work until the app is running again.

To relaunch PinPoint, open it from the **Start menu** by searching for "PinPoint", or double-click the shortcut on your desktop if you created one during installation.
