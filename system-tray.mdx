PinPoint lives in the Windows system tray — the notification area at the right end of the taskbar. The tray icon gives you a live view of all pinned windows and lets you manage them without using the hotkey.

## Opening the tray menu

Right-click the PinPoint icon in the system tray to open the menu. The menu shows:

- A list of all currently pinned windows (click any entry to unpin that window)
- **Unpin All** — removes Always-on-Top from every pinned window at once
- **Settings** — opens the PinPoint Settings window
- **Quit** — exits PinPoint

## Viewing pinned windows

Each pinned window appears in the tray menu by its title. If no windows are pinned, the list is empty.

## Opening settings from the tray

Right-click the PinPoint tray icon and choose **Settings** to open the Settings window. From Settings you can change your hotkey, adjust the border, change the theme, and more. The exact appearance of Settings may vary slightly depending on your version of Windows and system configuration.

## Quitting PinPoint

Right-click the tray icon and choose **Quit** to close PinPoint. Once quit, your hotkey will no longer work until you relaunch the app. PinPoint will relaunch automatically the next time you log in if the auto-start setting is enabled.

<Tip>
  If the tray icon is not visible, click the **^** arrow at the far right of your taskbar to reveal hidden icons. You can drag the PinPoint icon to the visible area to keep it accessible.
</Tip>
