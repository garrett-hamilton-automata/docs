Appearance settings control how the PinPoint Settings window looks and whether PinPoint plays sound effects when you pin or unpin a window.

## Theme

Choose from three theme options:

- **Light** — light background with dark text
- **Dark** — dark background with light text
- **System** — follows your Windows system theme (light or dark)

## Background color preset

Choose a background color preset for the PinPoint Settings window. Presets are available in multiple colors to match your preference.

## Sound effects

PinPoint can play a short sound when you pin or unpin a window. Sound effects are **off by default**.

To enable them, open Settings and turn on the sound effects toggle. You can turn them back off at any time.

## How to change appearance settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Right-click the PinPoint tray icon and choose **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Appearance section">
    Navigate to the Appearance section in Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your preferences">
    Select your theme, background color preset, and sound effects preference. Changes apply immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>
