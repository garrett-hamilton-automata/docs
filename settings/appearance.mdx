---
title: "Customize PinPoint's theme, background, and sounds"
description: "Change PinPoint's color theme (light, dark, or system), background color preset, and optional sound effects to match your preferences."
---

Appearance settings control how the PinPoint Settings window looks and how PinPoint provides feedback when you pin or unpin a window. You can choose a color theme that suits your environment, pick a background color for the Settings window, and turn sound effects on or off. All changes take effect immediately and save automatically.

## Theme

PinPoint supports three theme options that control the overall color scheme of the Settings window.

| Option | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Light** | Always uses a light color scheme. |
| **Dark** | Always uses a dark color scheme. |
| **System** | Follows your Windows color mode setting. |

The default theme is **System**. On Windows 11, choosing System means PinPoint automatically switches between light and dark as you change your OS appearance setting — no action required on your part.

To change the theme:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Right-click the PinPoint tray icon and choose **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a theme chip">
    Click **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** in the Theme section. The Settings window updates immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Background

The background setting changes the fill color of the PinPoint Settings window. Choose a color that complements your desktop or makes the window easier to spot when it's open.

Available options:

- **Default** — the standard background color for your current theme
- **Deep navy**
- **Slate gray**
- **Forest green**
- **Midnight purple**

To change the background:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Right-click the PinPoint tray icon and choose **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a background chip">
    Click any background color chip in the Background section. The Settings window background updates immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sound effects

When sound effects are enabled, PinPoint plays a short audio cue each time you pin or unpin a window using the global hotkey. Sound effects are off by default.

To toggle sound effects:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Right-click the PinPoint tray icon and choose **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle the checkbox">
    Check or uncheck **Enable sound effects** in the Sound section.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Sound effects are a subtle way to confirm a pin action if the border is hard to see on a particular window.
</Tip>
