Language and startup settings let you change the language PinPoint uses in its Settings window and control whether the Settings window opens automatically on launch.

## Language

PinPoint is available in 11 display languages, including English, Spanish, Chinese (Simplified), Japanese, and more.

To change the language:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Right-click the PinPoint tray icon and choose **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Language section">
    Navigate to the Language and startup section in Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your language">
    Choose your preferred language from the dropdown. The Settings window updates immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Startup mode

By default, PinPoint opens the Settings window when it launches for the first time so you can confirm your configuration. Once you have finished setting up, you can disable this:

1. Open PinPoint Settings.
2. Uncheck **Open settings when PinPoint starts**.
3. Close or send PinPoint to the tray.

From that point on, PinPoint starts silently in the background without opening any window.

PinPoint also adds itself to Windows startup automatically so it runs every time you log in. You can turn this off in the Language and startup section of Settings, or via **Windows Settings → Apps → Startup**.

<Note>
  Changing the language affects only the PinPoint Settings window. It does not affect Windows itself.
</Note>
