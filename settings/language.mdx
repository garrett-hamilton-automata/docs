---
title: "Change PinPoint's display language and startup mode"
description: "Set PinPoint's display language and control whether the settings window opens automatically when Windows starts and PinPoint launches."
---

Language and startup settings let you change the language PinPoint uses throughout its interface and decide whether the Settings window appears automatically each time Windows launches PinPoint in the background. Both settings are straightforward and take effect immediately when you change them.

## Language

PinPoint's interface — including menus, labels, and tray menu items — displays in your chosen language. The default is English.

Supported languages:

- English (`en`)
- Español — Spanish (`es`)
- 中文 (简体) — Simplified Chinese (`zh`)
- हिन्दी — Hindi (`hi`)
- 日本語 — Japanese (`ja`)
- Français — French (`fr`)
- Deutsch — German (`de`)
- Português — Portuguese (`pt`)
- Русский — Russian (`ru`)
- 한국어 — Korean (`ko`)
- Italiano — Italian (`it`)

To change the language:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Right-click the PinPoint tray icon and choose **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the language dropdown">
    Click the **Language** dropdown in the Language section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your language">
    Choose a language from the list. The Settings window and tray menu labels update immediately — no restart required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Open settings on startup

PinPoint can register itself as a Windows startup entry so it runs automatically in the background when you sign in. The **Open settings when PinPoint starts** setting controls what happens when that automatic startup entry fires.

When this option is **on** (the default), the Settings window opens each time PinPoint launches via its startup entry. When it is **off**, PinPoint starts silently in the background and your global hotkey is ready to use without any window appearing.

To change this setting:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Right-click the PinPoint tray icon and choose **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle the checkbox">
    Check or uncheck **Open settings when PinPoint starts** in the Startup section.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Launching PinPoint manually (from the Start menu, a shortcut, or the taskbar) always shows the Settings window regardless of this setting. This option only affects the automatic startup entry.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Turn this off once you're comfortable with PinPoint — it will start silently in the background, and your hotkey will work immediately without any window appearing.
</Tip>
