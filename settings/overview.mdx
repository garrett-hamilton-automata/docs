PinPoint exposes a small set of settings that control how it looks, sounds, and behaves. To open the Settings window, right-click the PinPoint icon in the system tray and choose **Settings**, or relaunch PinPoint from the Start menu. Every setting is organized into focused sections — hotkey, appearance, border, and language and startup — so you can find and change what you need quickly.

## Settings sections

### Hotkey

The global hotkey is the keyboard shortcut you press to pin or unpin the active window. The default chord is `Alt+P`. You can change it to any combination your system supports. See the [Hotkey](/hotkey) page for full instructions.

### Appearance

Appearance settings control PinPoint's color theme (Light, Dark, or System), the background color of the Settings window, and whether a sound plays when you pin or unpin a window. See [Customize PinPoint's appearance](/settings/appearance) for details.

### Border

The border is the colored frame PinPoint draws around every pinned window so you can tell at a glance which windows are on top. You can change the color, adjust the thickness, or turn the border off entirely. See [Customize the pinned window border](/settings/border) for details.

### Language and startup

Choose from 11 supported display languages and decide whether the Settings window opens automatically each time Windows starts PinPoint in the background. See [Language and startup settings](/settings/language) for details.

## Auto-save behavior

Settings save automatically as soon as you make a change — you do not need to do anything extra. A **Saved** indicator appears briefly next to the setting to confirm the change was written to disk. A manual **Save** button is also available if you prefer to confirm changes explicitly.
