## How PinPoint works

PinPoint runs quietly in your Windows system tray. When you press the global hotkey, the window that currently has focus is toggled to Always-on-Top — it floats above every other window until you press the hotkey again or unpin it from the tray menu.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install and launch">
    Run the PinPoint installer. The app starts automatically and places an icon in your system tray.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Focus a window">
    Click any window you want to keep on top — a browser tab, a video player, a chat app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Alt+P">
    Hit the default hotkey (or your custom one). A colored border appears around the window to confirm it's pinned.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get back to work">
    The pinned window stays on top. Press the hotkey again at any time to unpin it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key features

- **One-key pinning** — toggle Always-on-Top instantly without touching any menus
- **Visual confirmation** — a customizable colored border shows which windows are pinned
- **System tray control** — see all pinned windows and unpin any of them from the tray
- **Custom hotkey** — replace Alt+P with any shortcut that fits your workflow
- **Multilingual** — available in 11 languages including English, Spanish, Chinese, and more
- **Lightweight** — lives in the tray and uses minimal resources
